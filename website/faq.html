Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about speech therapy at North Dallas Speech.

Getting Started

Some signs that your child may benefit from a speech-language evaluation include: difficulty being understood by others, limited vocabulary for their age, trouble following directions, stuttering or disruptions in the flow of speech, or difficulty with social interactions. If you have concerns, we recommend scheduling a consultation so we can discuss your child's specific needs.

We provide assessment and treatment for children ages 3 through 18.

A physician referral is not required to begin speech therapy. However, some insurance companies may require one for reimbursement purposes. We recommend checking with your insurance provider.

The evaluation begins with a parent interview to gather background information about your child. We then use standardized assessments, informal measures, and observation to get a comprehensive picture of your child's communication skills. After the evaluation, we share our findings and recommendations with you and develop an individualized treatment plan.

During Therapy

Therapy sessions are typically 45–60 minutes, depending on your child's needs and goals. Session frequency is usually once or twice per week.

Absolutely! We encourage parent involvement. We regularly share strategies and activities that you can practice at home to support your child's progress. Parents are welcome to observe sessions and discuss goals with the therapist.

The duration of therapy varies for each child depending on the nature and severity of their communication needs. We regularly assess progress and adjust goals accordingly. We'll discuss estimated timelines during the evaluation process.

Payment & Insurance

We are an out-of-network provider. We provide a monthly superbill (detailed medical receipt) that you can submit to your insurance company for potential out-of-network reimbursement. We recommend contacting your insurance provider to understand your out-of-network benefits.

We accept all major credit cards and Health Savings Account (HSA) cards.
