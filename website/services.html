Our Services

Areas of Expertise

Articulation & Phonology

Articulation disorders involve difficulty producing specific speech sounds. Phonological disorders involve patterns of sound errors. Both can make a child difficult to understand.

  • Difficulty producing specific sounds (e.g., /r/, /s/, /l/)
  • Sound substitutions, omissions, or distortions
  • Reduced speech intelligibility

Receptive & Expressive Language

Language disorders can affect a child's ability to understand what others say (receptive) and/or express their own thoughts and ideas (expressive).

  • Difficulty following directions
  • Limited vocabulary or word-finding difficulties
  • Trouble forming sentences or telling stories
  • Difficulty understanding questions or concepts

Stuttering & Fluency Disorders

Stuttering is a communication disorder that affects the flow of speech. Our therapists have specialized training in evidence-based fluency therapy approaches.

  • Repetitions of sounds, syllables, or words
  • Prolongations or blocks during speech
  • Physical tension or secondary behaviors
  • Avoidance of speaking situations

Social Communication

Social communication skills are essential for building friendships and navigating social situations. We help children understand and use the "unwritten rules" of communication.

  • Difficulty with conversation skills
  • Trouble understanding nonverbal cues
  • Challenges with perspective-taking
  • Difficulty making and keeping friends
