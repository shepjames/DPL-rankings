Our Story

About North Dallas Speech

Dedicated to helping children communicate with confidence since 2014.

Our Mission

North Dallas Speech was founded with a simple but powerful goal: to provide high-quality, personalized speech therapy that helps children maximize their communication skills. We believe that every child deserves to express themselves, connect with others, and reach their full potential.

Our practice provides assessment and treatment for children ages 3–18 with a wide range of speech and language disorders. We take a family-centered approach, ensuring that parents and caregivers are active partners in the therapy process.

Meet Your Therapists

Photo: Laura Sheppard

Laura Sheppard

M.S., CCC-SLP · Owner & Therapist

Laura started North Dallas Speech in 2014 after working as a hospital and public school-based speech-language pathologist. She has a passion for supporting people on their communication journey and is especially dedicated to helping children and teens who stutter.

Laura received her Master of Science degree in Communication Disorders from The University of Texas at Dallas and her Bachelor of Science from The University of Texas at Austin. She is nationally certified by the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA) and licensed to provide speech therapy services in Texas and Massachusetts.

Her strong interest in fluency disorders led her to become a member of ASHA's Special Interest Group 4 on Fluency and Fluency Disorders. She is also a Co-Chapter Leader of the National Stuttering Association's Teens Who Stutter support group in North Dallas.

Emily Davis

M.S., CCC-SLP · Therapist

Emily specializes in working with young children with social communication deficits, receptive and expressive language disorders, and articulation/phonological deficits.

She brings warmth and creativity to every session, using play-based and evidence-based techniques to help children build their communication skills in a fun, engaging environment.
