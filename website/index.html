Welcome to North Dallas Speech

Where Every Child's Communication Journey Matters

Since 2014, North Dallas Speech has been dedicated to providing high-quality, personalized speech therapy to help children maximize their communication skills. We believe every child deserves to be heard and understood.

Our experienced, certified speech-language pathologists create individualized treatment plans in a warm, supportive environment where children can thrive.

What We Offer

Our Areas of Expertise

We provide assessment and treatment for a wide range of speech and language disorders.

Articulation & Phonology

Helping children produce speech sounds correctly and clearly so they can be understood by others.

Language Disorders

Supporting children with understanding and using language to express their thoughts, needs, and ideas.

Stuttering & Fluency

Specialized therapy to help children and teens who stutter communicate with greater ease and confidence.

Social Communication

Building skills for social interaction, conversation, and understanding nonverbal communication cues.
