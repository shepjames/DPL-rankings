## Networking Strategy by Job Match Type

### If the match is an in-house AI governance / privacy role:
1. Lead with **Eudia ecosystem contacts** — they're CLOs at Fortune 500s who are hiring for exactly these roles
2. Tap **Farrah Pepper** — deep network across legal innovation leaders globally
3. Check **ACC-DFW** chapter for local Dallas connections

### If the match is a legal operations / legal tech leadership role:
1. Lead with **CLOC board members** — they run legal ops at major companies
2. Tap **Ironclad advisory board peers** — legal ops leaders at top companies
3. **Farrah Pepper** again — she built legal innovation at Marsh McLennan

### If the match is at a law firm (partner/of counsel in AI practice):
1. **Rob Beard** — former BigLaw (Shearman, Weil Gotshal) now in-house, can connect both ways
2. **Omar Haroun** — Eudia works with law firms and just launched Eudia Counsel
3. **Marissa Lefland** — current law firm connection in aviation/AI space

### If the match is a GC / Deputy GC step-up role:
1. **Rob Beard** — made the jump to CLO at Coherent; can advise and refer
2. **Gary Hood** (Duracell GC), **Mark Smolik** (DHL CLO) — fellow Eudia GCs
3. **Dan Mascaro** (former CLO, Progressive) — Eudia Counsel advisor, likely well-connected to GC openings
